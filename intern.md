## CS Undergraduate Teaching intern scheme

UCL Computer Science is offering internships that will allow undergraduates to gain skills and experience by supporting
the teaching of a range of computer science modules.

### Eligibility & constraints

The scheme is open to current second, third and fourth year students in the UCL department of Computer Science.

You must be eligible to work in the UK during the period of the internship. If you are on a visa, your visa must cover
the full duration of the internship. UCL is unable to issue a visa for this scheme. You must comply with relevant visa
regulations.

Undergraduate intern hours are capped at total of 4 hours per teaching week in each of term 1 and term 2. Preparation,
where required, should be included in this.

Typical for a module are likely to be:

- Support 1 x 1 hour class per week (10 hours) + 50% preparation time (5 hours) = 15 hours
- Support 1 x 2 hour class per week (20 hours) + 50% preparation time (10 hours) = 30 hours

Internships must be taken outside the timetabled course requirements.

Payment is made through Unitemps
at [UCL spine point 5](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/human-resources/sites/human_resources/files/2023_-_24_ucl_non-clinical_grade_structure_with_spinal_points_december_2023_update_1.4.pdf).
This is currently £12.89 per hour including London allowance.

### Process

1. Check you are eligible to apply.
2. Find
   a [vacancy](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbxy3jRljUsf5ynnCYh8bpXG19pHqBldXhgrBYhocHMRLGI2aa0iO18SbsvHPSMQT4aH/exec).
3. Submit an application form for each vacancy you wish to apply for by the deadlines shown
   in [Key Dates](./keydates.html).
4. Please wait to be contacted by the hiring module leader who will discuss your application with you. If you are hired,
   you will receive an email confirmation from csc.postgradta@ucl.ac.uk.
5. If you are hired then you must:
    1. Create an account at www.unitemps.com.
    2. Accept the assignment (a link will be sent to you by email from Unitemps).
    3. Complete a right to work (RTW) check with the Unitemps team before you can start working. You cannot undertake
       any work until the RTW has been checked.
    4. Complete a Payroll form from Unitemps with bank details and tax information.
    5. Unitemps will provide you with information on how to complete timesheets to record your work. Timesheets will be
       authorised by <csc.postgradta@ucl.ac.uk>.
