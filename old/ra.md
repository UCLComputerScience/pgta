## PGTA process for UCL Research Fellows (RF) / Research Associates (RA)

### Summary

**All RF/RAs are asked to contribute 40 hours of teaching to
the [PGTA vacancies](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbzz2en51FJDuL9udZgfhFb2NvQ4PmDpp4pU4Hn-b757eukx4Jqjhib_8PCaG49rBQBQFg/exec)
by filling the [form](https://forms.gle/1trseuvYNAHdjeeK9).**

### Process steps

Contribution to teaching (TAing) is considered an integral part of your work at UCL. You are expected to get involved
and make things better for our student Computer Scientists!

It might be surprising to an RA/RF that they have to apply for PGTA work given this is an expected part of their duties.
We need to consider a fair recruitment practice for all. If, for example, there is more than one applicant for a single
position they must be considered in accordance with UCL’s fair recruitment policy.

TA recruitment proceeds as follows:

------

#### 1) Apply

Check
the [vacancies](https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbzz2en51FJDuL9udZgfhFb2NvQ4PmDpp4pU4Hn-b757eukx4Jqjhib_8PCaG49rBQBQFg/exec).

To apply, please fill the [form](https://forms.gle/1trseuvYNAHdjeeK9).

More info about each module see the [module catalogue](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/module-catalogue/).

To check the scheduled classes see the [UCL timetable](https://timetable.ucl.ac.uk/tt/homePage.do). Use the Custom
Timetable option and change the year to the next academic year (see top right of the screen).

Each RF/RA is being asked to contribute 40 TA hours total per academic year. Undergraduate modules are a priority, and
need
your help the most.

#### 2) Get an offer

[Departmental teaching support coordinator](mailto:csc.postgradta@ucl.ac.uk) will inform you if your application was
considered.

Under no circumstances should Module Leads contact applicants or vice versa directly to appoint them to modules, without
following the steps above.

#### 3) Qualify

All PGTAs must have completed relevant training. While RF/RAs are not eligible to take the Arena Gateway workshop,
development opportunities for postdocs and other teaching assistants are available
through [UCL Arena Open](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/teaching-learning/professional-development/arena-fellowship).
